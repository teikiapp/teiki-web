Privacy Policy

Teiki is built for private subscription tracking.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Overview

Teiki helps you track bills, subscriptions, reminders, and payment history. This policy explains how CapyDev handles information in the Teiki apps and on this website. You do not need a Teiki account to use the app.

Information You Add

You can enter subscription names, prices, billing cycles, due dates, tags, notes, reminder settings, and payment history into the app.

This information is used to provide app functionality such as tracking upcoming bills, calculating totals, organizing subscriptions, and showing reminders.

Local Storage

Teiki is designed as a local-first app. The bills, subscriptions, and settings you enter are stored on your device. Optional iCloud Sync on iOS can also store subscription data and supported settings in your iCloud account. Purchase services and diagnostics are described separately below.

iCloud Sync

On iOS, enabling iCloud Sync sends subscription data and supported settings to Apple's iCloud services so they can sync with other supported Apple devices using the same Apple Account. Android does not use iCloud Sync. Apple handles iCloud data under its Privacy Policy.

App Lock authentication stays on your device. Native iOS versions (2.0 and later) use system authentication, such as Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode; Teiki does not receive your biometric data or device passcode. App Lock options differ on Android and older iOS versions.

Notifications

Teiki uses notification permission to remind you about bills and subscriptions. Reminder notifications are used for app functionality and are not used for advertising or tracking.

Diagnostics

Native iOS versions (2.0 and later) do not include Sentry or send crash reports to Sentry. Android and older iOS versions use Sentry to report crashes and errors so we can find and fix stability issues. Reports may include device type, operating system and app versions, error messages, stack traces, and related diagnostic context.

We use these reports for troubleshooting, not advertising or product personalization. Sentry processes reports under its Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Teiki offers optional paid upgrades through the App Store on iOS and Google Play on Android. Available upgrades vary by platform and app version. Your purchase status is used to unlock features and restore purchases; payment details are handled by the store.

RevenueCat manages purchase validation, feature access, and restores. It receives purchase history and a randomly generated anonymous app user ID, along with app and device information needed to provide its service. RevenueCat also provides purchase reporting and analytics. We do not use purchase information for advertising. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Your Data and Deletion

You can delete archived items or use Reset all app data in Settings to remove your tracked bills, subscriptions, and payment history from the app. When iCloud Sync is enabled, deletions can also sync to your other connected devices. Turning sync off does not itself delete data already stored in iCloud.

Resetting the app does not cancel or refund purchases, or erase records held by purchase, diagnostic, or support providers. For questions about information you have sent us or requests to delete it, contact the email address below.

Website and Support

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may receive connection details, such as your IP address and browser information, when your browser requests a page or font. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Google Privacy Policy.

If you suggest a service, the service name and any website, region, email address, or notes you provide are sent through Formspree so we can review your suggestion and follow up if needed. Email is optional. Formspree may also process technical request information for security and spam prevention; see its Privacy Policy.

The form stores the time of your last successful submission in your browser's local storage to limit repeat submissions for one hour. This is not an advertising identifier; you can remove it by clearing this site's browser data.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments through our email provider, Microsoft Outlook. We use them to respond to your request. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Changes will be posted on this page.
